# Art Style Guide

All new visual assets and image prompts for this project should follow this direction:

- Style target: Hades-like hand-drawn 2D roguelike illustration, Supergiant-inspired, art direction reference: Jen Zee.
- Linework: bold, thick, clean black ink outlines on characters, props, environment elements, and tile silhouettes. No blurry edges.
- Shading and lighting: high-contrast dramatic cel-shading with sharp shadow boundaries, hand-drawn hatch marks, and chiaroscuro lighting.
- Palette: saturated jewel tones, especially ruby red, deep purple, emerald green, acid green, bright pink, and cyan accents over dark moody backgrounds.
- Perspective: isometric 2.5D top-down game asset perspective.
- Asset background: isolated on a clean solid white background when exported as a sprite or prop for easy transparency cutting.

Negative prompt to append to every generated asset prompt:

```text
3D render, photorealistic, realistic shading, soft shadows, gradient background, blurry lines, flat vector illustration.
```
